I'm a researcher in the [Human-Centered Computing (HCC) Lab](https://hcc.hanyang.ac.kr/), Department of Artificial Intelligence at Hanyang University, working with Prof. Kyungsik Han.

My research sits at the intersection of large language models and human-centered AI. I'm interested in how faithfully LLMs represent human values and opinions when they're used to substitute for or simulate human participants — and where they systematically diverge. Recent work audits **ethical value misalignment** in persona-injected, LLM-based human simulation across countries and models, and examines **topic-specific bias** and inter-model differences when LLMs are used as stand-ins for survey respondents.
